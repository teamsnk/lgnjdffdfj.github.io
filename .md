# Little Garden — review notes (mistakes found and what was done)

## Content errors in the documents
1. **Wrong statistic (Part 1, 2.5).** "73% of survey respondents had lost plants from forgetting to water." In the survey summary, 73.3% is the share who *want watering reminders*. Lost a plant = 89.7%. Forgetting to water = 46.7%. The site uses the correct figures.
2. **Mixed denominators.** Several percentages (82.8%, 89.7%, 79.3%) only work as x/29, while others (73.3%, 46.7%, 43.3%) work as x/30. Check the raw responses and state one n, or say "29–30 valid responses per item".
3. **Part 1 vs Part 2 do not match.** Part 1 tests "create an account" and shows a login flow; the final prototype has no account screens. Part 1 refers to Figure 8 where Figure 11 is meant.
4. **Survey is about air pollution, report is about plant care.** The survey and storyboards are built around air quality and a toddler's health, but the report barely mentions it. The site reconciles this: air quality is the motivation, care is the barrier.
5. **Methodology gaps the tutor flagged.** No recruitment criteria, age or gender breakdown, no interview analysis method (e.g. thematic analysis), no interview quotes. Export the Google Form responses and add these.
6. **Design-system document.** "Black" swatch is labelled #007537 (that is Primary Green). H1 and H2 say "Weight: Regular" but display bold. Layout panel is nearly empty.
7. **Accessibility claims.** Doc says "Most elements are easy to see". Audit: white on Tertiary Green 2.22:1 (banners, Done chips, Healthy chip), #A4A4A4 text 2.49:1, white on Terracotta 2.52:1 all fail WCAG AA.
